House rules

Throw
Swipe up. Angle is aim, length is power.
Drop
Lands on their half and they don't catch it: +1.
Catch
Tap the die while its ring is green.
FIFA
Only when the die goes off the table untouched, including a throw that goes long: tap it just before it hits the floor to kick it up, then tap again to catch it. +1 for you. Fumble a catch and it’s a drop.
Plink
Off the side of a cup: +2 if they drop it, nothing if they catch it. It comes off fast, so it’s much harder to catch.
Plop
In the cup: +3. First to 7 wins.
Power-ups
Earn one for every Plop (hold up to 2). Aim Guide and Double Down are used on your throw; Sticky Hands and Shaky Hands while they line up theirs.
