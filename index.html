Throw
Swipe up. Angle is aim, length is power.
Drop
Lands on their half and they don't catch it: +1.
Catch
Tap the die while its ring is green.
FIFA
Only when the die goes off the table untouched, including a throw that goes long or wide on your half: tap it just before it hits the floor to kick it up, then tap again to catch it. +1 for you. Fumble a catch and it’s a drop.
Plink
Off the side of a cup: +2 if they drop it, nothing if they catch it. It comes off fast, so it’s much harder to catch.
Plop
In the cup: +3. First to 7 or 11 wins, you pick.
Modes
Host a 1v1 or 2v2 game, public (listed under Open games) or private (code only). In 2v2 teammates take turns throwing and either one can catch.
Redemption
When someone hits the winning score, the other side gets one last shot. Plop it and the score goes back to what it was before the winning throw.
Contact
Questions, bugs or someone being abusive? Email matthiasgomez26@gmail.com. Privacy policy
Levels
Every finished game earns XP (more for a win). Every 5 levels unlocks a new arena, each bigger and louder than the last. Sign in to keep your level on every device.
Chat
Tap any message to report it or block that player. Blocked players’ messages, games and challenges are hidden.
Power-ups
Earn one for every Plop (hold up to 2). Aim Guide and Double Down are used on your throw. Money Cup and Shaky Hands can be used any time and affect their next throw.
Ranks
Win online games to earn rank points (+25, plus a bonus for every Plop); a loss costs 12. Beating the CPU and placing in tournaments earn some too. Ranks: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Elite, Champion, Unreal. Points reset each season.
Tournaments
Real players only. A new Minor Cup every day (up to 8 players, 3 entries a day) and Majors on set weekends of the season (up to 16 players, final to 11, 2 entries). Check in under Compete; the first player in can start it once 3 are in, or it starts itself when full. Matches start automatically. Lose once and you’re out; a no-show for 45 seconds forfeits. Winners get power-ups for their bank and a new dye.
Bank
Prize power-ups are saved to your account. You can use one banked power-up per game, in any mode.
Dyes
Change your die color under Play. Everyone sees your color when you throw.
