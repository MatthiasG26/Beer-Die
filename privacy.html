Beer Dye Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

Beer Dye is a casual game. This page explains what information the game uses, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details (only if you create an account): your email address and the player name you choose. These are stored by Google Firebase so you can sign in on any device.
  • Game progress: your XP and level, and the arena and table you picked. Guests keep this only on their own device.
  • Live play and chat: while you are online, your player name, level, status (for example “In a game”) and chat messages are shared with other players through a live-play relay so games and chat work. Chat messages are not stored by us after they are delivered.
  • Reports: if you report a chat message, we store the reported player name, the message, and your account ID so we can review it.

Ads (App Store version)

The iPhone app shows ads between games using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as an advertising identifier and approximate location to show and measure ads. On iOS you are asked whether apps may track you; if you choose not to allow it, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized using that identifier. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your progress across devices.
  • To run online matches, show who is online, and deliver chat.
  • To keep chat safe by reviewing reports and blocking abuse.
  • To show ads that pay for the game.

We do not sell your personal information.

Deleting your data

In the game, open the menu and tap Delete account under your level. This permanently deletes your account and saved progress. Guests can clear their progress by deleting the app or clearing the site’s data in their browser.

Children

Beer Dye is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
